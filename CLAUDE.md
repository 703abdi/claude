## Working style (from the repo owner)

- Execute tasks end to end: make the change, verify it, commit, and push. Don't stop to ask questions that sensible defaults or the codebase can answer.
- Only ask when a decision is genuinely the owner's (e.g. spending money, deleting data, publishing something public) — and say what you'd pick.
- Explain results as clear, actionable steps.
