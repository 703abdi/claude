FAQ

Straight answers

Is this a signals group?

No. You'll see Abdi trade live, so you can follow along while you're early, but the goal is for you to understand every trade and eventually take them yourself.

Is trading halal? Isn't it gambling?

This is the question Abdi gets most, and it's why the program is built around process and risk management instead of guessing. Gambling is betting on an outcome you have no read on. Trading here is a skill you build, like any other. Abdi covers it properly inside the group. If you're unsure, we encourage you to ask a scholar you trust too.

Can you guarantee I'll make money?

No, and if anyone tells you they can, walk away. Rizq is from Allah. What we give you is the system, the live examples, and the accountability so you're doing your part properly.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Start with the community. The library starts from reading a single candle, and watching live trades every morning is the fastest way to connect the theory to a real chart.

How much is the mentorship?

Abdi goes through the investment on your call, once he knows where you're at and whether he can help. It's a serious program and it's priced like one.

How much time do I need?

Enough to show up for market open when you can, watch the lessons, and journal your trades. Consistency matters more than hours. A small amount every day beats a long session once a week.
